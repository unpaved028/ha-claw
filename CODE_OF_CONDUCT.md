# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

HA-Claw adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text is authoritative; this page summarises it and records
how to report a problem.

In short: everyone who takes part in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions —
is expected to make participation a harassment-free experience for everybody, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings about context.
- Accept responsibility, apologise to those affected by mistakes, and learn from them.

Behaviour that does not belong here is defined in
[section 1 of the Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#our-standards).
Maintainers will remove, edit or reject contributions that violate it, and will explain
moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests and
discussions — and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/unpaved028/ha-claw/security/advisories/new)
(it works for conduct reports too and is not publicly visible) or by contacting
[@unpaved028](https://github.com/unpaved028) directly.

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy and security of
the reporter is respected. Reports are handled by the project maintainer, who will recuse
themselves if they are personally involved.

## Enforcement

Consequences follow the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a private correction first, then a warning, then a temporary ban, and a permanent ban only
for sustained or severe violations.
